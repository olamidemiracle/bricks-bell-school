Welcome

A Warm, Safe Place for Young Minds to Grow

Bricks Bell Nursery & Primary School is a private educational institution located along Ikorodu Road in Irawo Bus Stop, Lagos. We offer a complete early-years and primary pathway, guiding children from their first steps in the classroom through to a strong finish in primary school.

  • Our mission: build strong academic, social and moral foundations
  • Our vision: a trusted school for Irawo Bus Stop families
