Academics

A continuous learning pathway from Nursery through Primary school.

Ages 2 – 5

Nursery School

Our Nursery classes give children a gentle, play-based start to school life, building early language, numeracy, motor and social skills.

  • Letter and number recognition
  • Storytelling, songs and rhymes
  • Guided play and social skills
  • Creative and motor-skill activities
Ages 6 – 11

Primary School

Our Primary classes build on the Nursery foundation with a structured curriculum covering the core subjects pupils need to progress confidently.

  • English Language & Literacy
  • Mathematics
  • Basic Science & Social Studies
  • Moral instruction & character building
Our Approach

How We Help Children Learn

Small, Manageable Classes

A classroom size that allows teachers to give pupils individual attention.

Step-by-Step Progression

Each term builds on the last, so pupils move up with a solid grasp of the basics.

Parent Partnership

Regular communication with parents to support each child's progress.
