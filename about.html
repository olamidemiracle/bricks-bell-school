About Bricks Bell

Getting to know our school, our mission and the values that guide everything we do.

Our Story

A Private School Rooted in Fadeyi, Lagos

Bricks Bell Nursery & Primary School is a private educational institution located at 77 Ondo Street, off Ikorodu Road, Fadeyi, Lagos. As a neighbourhood school offering both Nursery and Primary classes, we serve families looking for a nearby institution where their children can receive quality early education close to home.

Just like the building blocks in our name, we believe every child's education should be laid brick by brick — steady, structured and secure — so that each stage of learning supports the next.

Get in Touch

Our Mission

To provide a safe, structured and encouraging environment where every child in our care develops strong academic, social and moral foundations for lifelong learning and success.

Our Vision

To be a trusted neighbourhood school in Fadeyi and its environs, known for nurturing confident, well-rounded pupils who are ready for the next stage of their education.
