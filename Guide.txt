Build a modern, beautiful, child-friendly website for a Nursery and Primary School.

The website should immediately feel like it is designed for children, but it must also look professional, trustworthy, and appealing to parents.

### Design Style

* Use a playful, colorful, cheerful visual style.
* Use bright but soft colors such as sky blue, yellow, orange, green, purple, and other child-friendly colors.
* Use rounded cards, buttons, sections, and friendly shapes.
* Add subtle playful illustrations such as books, pencils, stars, clouds, school buses, ABC letters, numbers, crayons, backpacks, and children learning.
* Use friendly, modern typography that is easy to read.
* Add tasteful animations and hover effects, but don't overdo them.
* Use rounded corners and soft shadows.
* Make the website feel energetic and welcoming rather than corporate.
* The design should work beautifully on phones, tablets, and desktops.

### Target Audience

The website has two main audiences:

1. Parents looking for a good school for their children.
2. Children who should find the website fun and welcoming.

Parents should immediately be able to understand:

* What the school offers
* Why they should choose the school
* The school's academic approach
* The classes available
* School activities
* Facilities
* How to contact the school
* How to apply/enroll their child

### Homepage

Create an engaging homepage with:

1. A playful hero section

   * Strong headline welcoming parents and children.
   * Short description about the school.
   * Primary CTA: "Enroll Your Child"
   * Secondary CTA: "Explore Our School"
   * Include a cheerful school/children illustration or high-quality image.

2. Quick school statistics

   * Years of experience
   * Number of students
   * Number of teachers
   * Classes/programs

3. About the School

   * Short introduction
   * Mission and vision
   * "Learn More" button

4. Our Classes
   Display nursery and primary classes in attractive cards.
   For example:

   * Nursery 1
   * Nursery 2
   * Nursery 3
   * Primary 1
   * Primary 2
   * Primary 3
   * Primary 4
   * Primary 5
   * Primary 6

5. Why Choose Us?
   Highlight things such as:

   * Qualified teachers
   * Safe learning environment
   * Strong academic foundation
   * Character development
   * Extracurricular activities
   * Modern learning facilities

6. School Activities
   Showcase activities such as:

   * Sports
   * Music
   * Arts and crafts
   * Excursions
   * Cultural activities
   * Science activities

7. Facilities
   Showcase classrooms, playground, library, computer room, science facilities, etc.

8. Parent Testimonials
   Include attractive testimonial cards from parents.

9. Call-to-Action section
   Encourage parents to start the admission process.

10. Footer
    Include:

* School logo/name
* Quick links
* Contact information
* Address
* Phone number
* Email
* Social media links

### Navigation

Create a clear navigation bar with:

* Home
* About Us
* Academics
* Classes
* Activities
* Facilities
* Admissions
* Gallery
* Contact

Include a prominent "Apply Now" or "Enroll Now" button.

### Important UX Requirements

* The website must be easy for parents to navigate.
* Important information should never be difficult to find.
* Make the admission/enrollment process obvious.
* Use clear call-to-action buttons.
* Make the website accessible and readable.
* Optimize the design for mobile devices.
* Avoid making it look like a generic corporate business website.
* Avoid excessive animations that could make the website distracting.
* Keep the playful design polished and professional.

### Overall Feeling

When someone opens the website, they should immediately think:

"This is a fun, caring, safe, and high-quality school where my child can learn and grow."

Make the final design feel like a premium modern nursery and primary school website, combining a playful children's aesthetic with the trust and professionalism parents expect from a school.
