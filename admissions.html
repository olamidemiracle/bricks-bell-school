Admissions

Ready to enroll your child? Here's how the process works at Bricks Bell.

For up-to-date information on fees, available spaces and resumption dates, please call the school office directly on 0703 759 2007.
How to Apply

A Simple 4-Step Process

  1. Contact the School

    Call 0703 759 2007 or visit us at 77 Ondo St, Ikorodu Rd, Fadeyi, Lagos to enquire about admission.

  2. Book a Visit

    Tour the school compound and meet the staff to see if Bricks Bell is the right fit for your child.

  3. Submit Registration Details

    Provide your child's information and complete the school's registration formalities.

  4. Confirm Enrollment

    Complete any outstanding requirements and secure your child's place in their class.

Good to Know

General Requirements

Have the following ready when you contact the school office — exact requirements will be confirmed by the school.

Birth Certificate

Proof of the child's date of birth for age-appropriate class placement.

Previous Reports

Recent school reports or transfer records, where applicable.

Immunization Record

Up-to-date health and immunization information for the child.

Parent/Guardian Contact

Valid phone number and address for the parent or guardian.
